# High-Performance Composites, Engineering Polymers, and Ceramics Selection

## 1. Continuous Carbon Fiber Epoxy Prepreg (CFRP)
- **Density:** 1,550 kg/m³ (ultra-lightweight, 5x lighter than steel).
- **Mechanical Properties:** Young's modulus $E = 135\text{ GPa}$ (longitudinal), tensile yield strength $\sigma_y = 1,200\text{ MPa}$, ultimate tensile strength $UTS = 1,500\text{ MPa}$.
- **Ashby Performance Index:** Specific stiffness $M = E / \rho \approx 87\text{ GPa/(g/cm³)}$ and bending stiffness $M = E^{1/2} / \rho \approx 7.5\text{ (GPa)}^{1/2}\text{/(g/cm³)}$. Unrivaled among structural engineering materials.
- **Manufacturing & Curing:** Prepreg plies laid in balanced symmetric schedules $[0^\circ/\pm 45^\circ/90^\circ]_s$ inside precision molds, vacuum debulked, and autoclave cured at $120^\circ\text{C} - 180^\circ\text{C}$ under 6 bar hydrostatic pressure.
- **Applications:** Formula 1 aerodynamic monocoques, competition Tour de France bicycle frames, aircraft wing skins, and lightweight drone airframes.

## 2. Polyetheretherketone (PEEK)
- **Density:** 1,300 kg/m³.
- **Thermal & Chemical Resistance:** Heat deflection temperature $HDT = 250^\circ\text{C}$ (continuous service up to $260^\circ\text{C}$). Unaffected by almost all organic solvents, acids, fuels, and superheated steam (autoclave sterilizable).
- **Mechanical Properties:** Young's modulus $E = 3.8\text{ GPa}$, yield strength $\sigma_y = 95\text{ MPa}$, fatigue strength limit $60\text{ MPa}$.
- **Applications:** Metal-replacement impellers in aggressive chemical processing, high-heat semiconductor test sockets, aerospace cable conduits, and FDA-cleared spinal fusion cages.

## 3. Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU 95A): Elastic Shock Absorption
- **Density:** 1,210 kg/m³.
- **Hyperelasticity:** Young's modulus $E = 0.05\text{ GPa}$ (50 MPa), elongation at break $>500\%$, Izod notched impact toughness $120\text{ kJ/m²}$.
- **Ashby Strain Energy:** Governed by $M = \sigma_y^2 / E$. Maximizes reversible elastic deformation, absorbing drop kinetic energy without fracture or plastic deformation.
- **Applications:** Smartphone protective cases, drone landing bumpers, vibration isolation mounts, flexible couplings, and shoe soles.

## 4. Technical Ceramics: Alumina (Al2O3 99.5%)
- **Density:** 3,900 kg/m³.
- **Hardness & Stiffness:** Young's modulus $E = 370\text{ GPa}$, Mohs hardness 9.0 (second only to diamond), compressive strength $>2,500\text{ MPa}$, dielectric strength $>15\text{ kV/mm}$.
- **Thermal Stability:** Maximum service temperature up to $1,600^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Applications:** High-voltage electrical insulators, wear-resistant abrasive sandblasting nozzles, ceramic ball bearings, and spark plug insulators.

## 5. Polyoxymethylene / Acetal (POM Delrin): Low-Friction Tribology
- **Density:** 1,410 kg/m³.
- **Tribological Mechanics:** Dynamic coefficient of friction $\mu \approx 0.15$ against steel. High natural lubricity eliminates the need for external grease in clean mechanisms.
- **Mechanical Properties:** Young's modulus $E = 2.8\text{ GPa}$, yield strength $\sigma_y = 65\text{ MPa}$, fatigue limit $32\text{ MPa}$.
- **Applications:** Precision gears, sliding bushings, conveyor chain links, guitar picks, and automotive fuel sender units.
