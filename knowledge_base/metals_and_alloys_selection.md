# Engineering Metals and Structural Alloys: Material Selection Handbook

## 1. Titanium Alloys: Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5)
- **Density:** 4,430 kg/m³ (45% lighter than structural steel).
- **Mechanical Properties:** Young's modulus $E = 114\text{ GPa}$, yield strength $\sigma_y = 880\text{ MPa}$, ultimate tensile strength $UTS = 950\text{ MPa}$.
- **Fatigue Performance:** Fatigue limit $\sigma_{fatigue} = 510\text{ MPa}$ at $10^7$ cycles. Exceptional specific fatigue strength ($M = \sigma_{fatigue} / \rho$).
- **Biocompatibility & Corrosion:** Forms an instantaneous, self-healing, hermetic titanium dioxide ($\text{TiO}_2$) passivating oxide layer. Fully biocompatible according to ISO 10993, making it the premier choice for orthopedic hip and knee replacement implants.
- **Applications:** Aerospace airframe bulkheads, jet engine compressor blades, high-stress racing bicycle forks, deep-sea exploration subs, and biomedical surgical implants.

## 2. High-Conductivity Copper Alloys: Electrolytic Tough Pitch (C11000)
- **Density:** 8,940 kg/m³.
- **Thermal Properties:** Thermal conductivity $\lambda = 390\text{ W/(m·K)}$, specific heat capacity $c_p = 385\text{ J/(kg·K)}$.
- **Mechanical Properties:** Yield strength $\sigma_y = 200\text{ MPa}$, Young's modulus $E = 117\text{ GPa}$.
- **Physics of Heat Conduction:** Governed by Fourier's law ($q = -\lambda \nabla T$). When heat dissipation is steady-state, minimizing temperature rise $\Delta T$ requires maximizing $\lambda$. Copper outperforms aluminum by 2.3x and structural steel by nearly 10x.
- **Applications:** CPU heat sink baseplates, vapor chamber heat spreaders, high-power electronics cooling, electric motor windings, and industrial heat exchangers.

## 3. High-Strength Chromium-Molybdenum Steel: AISI 4140 (Quenched & Tempered)
- **Density:** 7,850 kg/m³.
- **Mechanical Properties:** Young's modulus $E = 205\text{ GPa}$, yield strength $\sigma_y = 655\text{ MPa}$, ultimate tensile strength $UTS = 850\text{ MPa}$.
- **Fatigue & Fracture Toughness:** Fatigue limit $\sigma_{fatigue} = 420\text{ MPa}$, Izod notched impact toughness $55\text{ kJ/m²}$. High fracture toughness $K_{IC} \approx 65\text{ MPa}\sqrt{\text{m}}$.
- **Applications:** Heavy-duty crane hoisting hooks, high-torque vehicle drive shafts, connecting rods, suspension bolts, and legendary "Chromoly" high-performance bicycle frame tubing.

## 4. Nickel Superalloys: Inconel 718
- **Density:** 8,190 kg/m³.
- **Thermal & Mechanical Limits:** Yield strength $\sigma_y = 1,100\text{ MPa}$ at room temperature; retains $>1,000\text{ MPa}$ at $650^\circ\text{C}$. Melting/solidus threshold $1,336^\circ\text{C}$, maximum continuous service temperature up to $700^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Creep Resistance:** Gamma-double-prime ($\gamma'' - \text{Ni}_3\text{Nb}$) precipitates prevent grain-boundary sliding under high thermal-mechanical stress.
- **Applications:** Rocket engine combustion chambers, jet engine high-pressure turbine disks, exhaust manifolds, and turbocharger turbine wheels.
